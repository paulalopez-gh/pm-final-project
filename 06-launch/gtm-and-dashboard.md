- **Your persona pull your M2 persona, this anchors your audience:** A discovery-driven viewer overwhelmed by StreamLine's trend-heavy catalog who leaves the platform to seek trusted film recommendations elsewhere before deciding what to watch.

## Set your goal, then your audience
- **Primary GTM goal awareness · engagement · conversion, pick one:** Conversion
- **Why this goal? what makes this the right goal for this feature right now:** "Why You'll Love This" is designed to change a specific user behavior, not create awareness. The core problem is that discovery-driven viewers browse but fail to confidently choose a film, leading them to leave StreamLine for external recommendation sources. The feature aims to increase Discovery-to-Play Conversion by helping users make faster, more confident viewing decisions at the moment of choice.
- **Target audience the specific segment your goal implies, be precise:** Persona 1: The Dedicated Cinephile
Role: A passionate film enthusiast who actively seeks high-quality, independent, and international cinema.
Goal: Discover meaningful films and curated recommendations without spending time searching across multiple platforms.
Friction: StreamLine's large catalog lacks trusted curation, forcing them to use Letterboxd, IMDb, or Rotten Tomatoes to find recommendations before returning to StreamLine to watch content.

Persona 2: The Discovery-Driven Subscriber
Role: A long-term StreamLine subscriber who values discovering great content more than having access to the largest library.
Goal: Quickly find films that match their tastes and broaden their cinematic horizons.
Friction: The platform does not provide a clear, editorially guided discovery experience, leading them to subscribe to specialized services such as MUBI or Criterion Channel for content discovery.

## Size your launch
- **Launch tier S (minimal) · M (targeted) · L (multi-channel) · XL (full GTM):** M (targeted)
- **Justification reach + revenue impact + what silence would risk:** "Why You'll Love This" is not a net-new product or a platform-wide change. It is a focused discovery improvement designed to increase Discovery-to-Play Conversion among a specific high-risk segment (Wanderers). The feature has meaningful retention and revenue potential because Wanderers have the lowest LTV ($8.40) and the highest discovery friction, but it does not justify a broad press or awareness campaign.
- **Is this a launch or a release? does it need go-to-market, or can it just ship?:** Its a realease and it can be sheaped with a meddium tier and owned channels.

## Choose your top three channels and plan assets
- **Channel 1 owned / earned / paid, and why it reaches your audience:** Owned → In-App Homepage Banner + Spotlight Entry Promotion

Why: Wanderers are already inside StreamLine but struggle to convert discovery into viewing. Promoting Spotlight at the moment they begin browsing directly intercepts the "leave StreamLine and search elsewhere" behavior.

Message: "Stop scrolling. Start watching. See why each film was selected for you."
- **Channel 2 owned / earned / paid, and why:** Owned → Spotlight Onboarding Tooltip / Product Walkthrough

Why: The value of "Why You'll Love This" is not immediately obvious. A lightweight onboarding flow educates users that recommendations now include trusted editorial explanations, reducing decision paralysis during the first discovery session.

Message: "Every Spotlight recommendation comes with a clear explanation, so you know why it's worth your time."
- **Channel 3 owned / earned / paid, and why:** Earned → Film & Cinema Communities (Letterboxd, Reddit r/movies, Film Newsletters)

Why: Our target audience already uses external discovery platforms for curation and recommendations. Sharing Spotlight's "editor-picked with explanation" approach where cinephiles already seek trusted discovery creates credibility and helps reposition StreamLine as a discovery destination.

Message: "A curated shelf of films with the reason each one was chosen, inside your streaming service."
