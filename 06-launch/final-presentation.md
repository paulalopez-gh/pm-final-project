# Final Project Presentation

> ★ Final submission, the deck you submit to the learning platform to qualify for certification.

Your final project is a single presentation, a self-contained HTML deck built from the six deliverable files in this repo, that tells the story of your product concept end-to-end.

## How to build it

1. Open the **Final Presentation Generator** (linked from the Module 6 deck). It comes pre-loaded with a worked example so you can see the shape, then **edit each card** or **upload the `.md` files you exported** from each module's builder to pre-populate it from this repo. Each deliverable file maps to a section of the deck:
   - **Problem Hook, Value Prop & Hypothesis** ← `01-product-thinking/problem-hook.md` (+ hypothesis from `03-analytics/`)
   - **Competitive Analysis & Journey Map** ← `02-discovery/competitive-and-journey.md`
   - **Prioritization, Roadmap, PRD & Prototype** ← `04-roadmap/roadmap-prd-prototype.md`
   - **Experimentation Plan** ← `05-experimentation/experimentation-plan.md`
   - **GTM Strategy & Success Dashboard** ← `06-launch/gtm-and-dashboard.md`
   - **Individual Insights** ← `06-launch/individual-insights.md`
2. Confirm your repo URL, copy the master prompt the generator builds, and run it in **Claude**, **ChatGPT**, or **Gemini** to produce a single-file HTML deck. _(Prefer slides? Paste the same content into Gamma or Canva instead.)_
3. Save the result as `06-launch/final-presentation.html`, commit it, and enable **GitHub Pages** (Settings → Pages) so your deck has a shareable link.
4. Review, then submit your **repo link** and your **presentation deck** to the learning platform within **7 days** of your cohort ending.

## The final showcase _(optional)_

During the last ~30 minutes of Module 6, three to five volunteers present (5 min each):

1. **Demo the core logic**: show your wireframes and how your concept addresses the user's needs.
2. **Defend your artifacts**: walk through one or two you're most proud of and the trade-offs behind them.
3. **Share your insights**: close with your friction points, key learnings, and aha moments.
